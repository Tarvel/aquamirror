Process & Transport Dynamics
💧 Fluid Mechanics (Friction Head Loss)

Frictional pressure drop is simulated using the Hazen-Williams equation:

h_f = 10.67 • L • Q1.85 / (C1.85 • D4.87)

Where Q is flow rate, D is diameter, and C is pipe roughness. Note how leaks increase velocity, driving pressure down.

⚖️ Material Conservation (Mass Balance)

DMAs calculate mass balances over rolling 30-minute intervals:

Accumulation = Inflow - Outflow - Loss

A continuous positive loss metric flags joint fractures or underground seepage before structural collapse.

🧪 Transport Phenomena (First-order Decay)

Residual Chlorine decay governed by reaction-diffusion transport:

Cl(t) = Cl0 • e-k • t

Water age determines safe chlorine levels. The twin flags stagnation nodes lacking active mixing.
